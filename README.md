**Neon D-Fence Remaster** is an exhilarating tower defense game for Windows that combines strategy with fast-paced action. Created by TnT Gamez, players are challenged to protect their motherboard from an AI invasion by strategically placing towers and crafting intricate mazes to thwart relentless enemies. The **latest version** introduces fresh gameplay elements and is packed with exciting features. 

With ten uniquely designed maps, each presenting distinct challenges, players can earn in-game currency such as "Ram" and "Data" to unlock and upgrade a diverse arsenal of ten tower types.
